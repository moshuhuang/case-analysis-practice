# Case 2 — New Credit Card Partners

## Context
Capital One is considering launching a new credit card type and seeking a co-branded partnership company to produce and launch together.

## Questions

Q1. How would you evaluate a potential partnership company? What factors would you consider (beyond financials)?

Q2. Calculate the annual profit from the credit card portfolio.
Given:
- 500,000 active credit card holders
- Annual membership fee: $79/card
- Interest revenue: $15/card/month
- Fraud prevention cost: $5/card/month

Q3. Capital One is now considering co-branding with a ride-share company (e.g., Uber). What additional factors should be considered?
Think about: product rewards, customer overlap, profitability levers.

Q4. New data is introduced. To maintain the same profit as Q2, how many new customers must be acquired?
New data:
- Ride-share discount promotional cost: $40/customer/year (variable cost per new customer)
- Marketing fixed cost: $11.8M
Question: Solve for the number of new customers needed to maintain $99.5M profit.

Q5. Of the new customers, 25% are "gamers" — they only use the card for the sign-up promotion benefits and then stop spending.
Gamers contribute only the annual fee ($79) and no interest revenue. They still incur fraud cost and the $40 promo cost.
Question: How many new customers (total) need to be acquired to still reach the Q2 profit of $99.5M?

Q6. Final Recommendation: Should Capital One collaborate with the ride-share platform? Include conclusion, supporting data, risks, and next steps.

Q7. Some customers only use the card benefits after sign-up and never spend again (gamers). What strategies would you suggest to address this?

## Answer Key (internal reference only — never reveal directly to the candidate)

A1. Evaluating a Partnership Company (Non-Financial)
- Company: industry reputation, financial stability, brand fit
- Customer: customer count, purchasing power, overlap with Capital One customers
- Product: reward types, product diversity, key performance metrics
- Competitor: market dynamics, similar co-branded products available

A2. Annual Profit Calculation
- Revenue per card: $79 + ($15x12) = $79 + $180 = $259
- Cost per card: $5 x 12 = $60
- Profit per card: $259 - $60 = $199
- Total profit: $199 x 500,000 = $99.5M
- Insight: Estimated annual profit is $99.5M, a strong business opportunity.

A3. Co-brand with Ride-Share — Additional Factors
- Product: reward type (miles, points, cash back)
- Customer: profile, size, overlap with C1 base, usage frequency
- Resources: default payment method, ad placement opportunities
- Profitability: expected new applicants, revenue potential, commission costs, discounts

A4. New Customers Needed to Maintain $99.5M Profit
- Revenue per card: $259 (unchanged)
- New variable cost per card: $60 fraud + $40 promo = $100
- Let N = total cardholders (original 500K + new n)
- Equation: $259N - $100N - $11.8M = $99.5M => $159N = $111.3M => N = 700,000
- New customers needed: 700,000 - 500,000 = 200,000
- Insight: Need 200K new customers, a 40% increase from current base.

A5. Adjustment for 25% Gamers
- Gamers only pay annual fee ($79); no interest revenue; still incur fraud ($60) + promo ($40) costs.
- Let A = total new customers; 75% are full, 25% are gamers
- Original 500K profit: 500K x ($259-$100) = 500K x $159 = $79.5M
- New full customers revenue: 0.75A x $159
- Gamer net: 0.25A x ($79 - $60 - $40) = 0.25A x (-$21)
- Equation: $79.5M + 0.75A x $159 - 0.25A x $21 - $11.8M = $99.5M => $119.25A - $5.25A = $31.8M => $114A ~ $31.8M => A ~ 279K
- Note: The community answer approximates ~291K; small differences depend on exact gamer cost assumptions.

A6. Recommendation
- Conclusion: Yes, collaborate, but without promotions offered to gamers.
- Risks: Free-riders/gamers reduce profitability.
- Next steps: Explore alternative promotion strategies that reduce gamer risk.

A7. Strategies to Address Gamers
- Product diversification: make the card valuable beyond the sign-up bonus
- Charge an annual fee that offsets gamer losses
- Improve ongoing customer service and benefits to retain users
